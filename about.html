About Us

Good food, shared at a table with room for everyone.

Our Story

Red Table began with a simple idea: food tastes better when it is
shared. We cook family recipes the way they were meant to be
made, slowly, with care, and with local ingredients.

From smoky party jollof rice to suya, pepper soup and moi moi,
every plate is made fresh, so you can taste the difference.
